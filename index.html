How EXCHORA Works

Why we built it: every semester, students finish a course with notes, books and slides that just sit unused — while juniors struggle for the same materials. EXCHORA turns that waste into exchange. No money, just knowledge.

1

Create your account

Sign up free with your email — it takes less than a minute. Your profile keeps your posts and saved exchanges in one place.

2

Post HAVE ⇄ WANT

Share what you have — notes, a book, slides, a question bank — and write what you need in return. That pair is one exchange post.

3

Find your match

Search and filter by subject, semester or type — or open Matches, where the engine pairs posts whose HAVE and WANT fit each other.

4

Exchange & learn

Tap Interested on a post to save it, connect with the poster, and swap — meet on campus for books, or share files for PDFs. Knowledge passed on.

About EXCHORA

EXCHORA is a student-to-student exchange platform. Instead of buying new materials or letting old ones gather dust, students swap notes, books, slides and question banks directly with each other — and talk, coordinate and build trust inside one community.

🎓

By students, for students

Built by a CSE student who watched seniors' materials go to waste while juniors searched everywhere for the very same things.

🔁

Exchange, not buying

No money changes hands. You give what you have, get what you need — and earn points and ratings that recognise your contribution.

💬

A real community

Discuss together on the Community wall, message any student privately, and coordinate your exchange — all inside EXCHORA.

Created by Khairun Nessa Mim — BSc in CSE, Southeast University, Dhaka, Bangladesh.
